60 din. Har din ek dhadkan, aur har dhadkan tumhare naam.

Pata hai Radha, 2 mahine pehle mujhe andaza bhi nahi tha ki ek insaan se milkar zindagi itni halki, itni khoobsurat lag sakti hai.

Ab mera din tumhare khayal se shuru hota hai aur raat tumhari awaaz par khatam. Beech ka sab kuch bas tumse dobara baat karne ka intezaar hai.

11 September ko jab ye safar shuru hua tha, tab mujhe nahi pata tha ki ye sirf ek shuruaat nahi, meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat mod hoga. Aaj peeche mudkar dekhta hoon to lagta hai jaise main tumse milne se pehle bas chal raha tha, jee to ab raha hoon.

Pyaar shor nahi karta.

Tumne mujhe yehi sikhaya. Pyaar tumhare “kha liya?” poochne mein hota hai, tumhari chhoti si muskaan mein, aur us sukoon mein jo sirf tumhare paas milta hai.

60 din ka matlab 1,440 ghante, aur 1,440 ghanton ka matlab 86,400 minute. Sach bolun to in 86,400 minute mein shayad hi koi minute gaya ho jab tumhara khayal na aaya ho. Kabhi bina wajah muskura deta hoon, aur kisi ko samajh nahi aata kyun. Mujhe pata hai kyun.

Jo main aksar keh nahi paata.

Kabhi-kabhi tumse baat karte hue main chup ho jaata hoon, aur tum sochti ho ki main kahin kho gaya. Sach ye hai ki main bas tumhe sun raha hota hoon, tumhari awaaz mein kho jaata hoon. Tumhari baatein, tumhari hasi, tumhara gussa bhi, mujhe sab pyaara lagta hai. Haan, gussa bhi.

Mujhe darr bhi lagta hai Radha, bas ek cheez ka: tumhe kabhi khone ka. Isliye main har din koshish karta hoon ki tum khush raho, mehfooz raho, aur tumhe kabhi ye lage hi nahi ki tum akeli ho. Jab tak main hoon, tum akeli nahi ho.

Tum mere liye ghar jaisi ho.

Duniya kitni bhi bhaage, main kitna bhi thak jaun, tumse baat karte hi sab thehar jaata hai. Ghar kisi jagah ka naam nahi hota. Ghar wo insaan hota hai jiske paas pahunchkar dil ko chain mil jaaye. Mere liye wo tum ho.

Mere waade, tumse.

  • Main tumhari baatein hamesha dhyaan se sunoonga, tab bhi jab tum nakhre mein ho.
  • Jab tum thak jaogi, main tumhara sahara banunga.
  • Tumhare sapnon par main kabhi hansunga nahi, unke saath chalunga.
  • Jhagde honge, par main tumhara haath kabhi nahi chhodunga.
  • Tumhari muskaan ko kabhi kam nahi hone dunga.

Aane wale kal mein, tum.

Mujhe tumhare saath bahut kuch dekhna hai. Subah ki chai, lambe safar, bina wajah ki baatein, chhoti-chhoti ladaiyaan aur unke baad ki sabse meethi manaai. Mujhe wo din bhi dekhne hain jab hum purani baatein yaad karke hansenge aur kahenge, dekho, shuruaat ke 2 mahine kaise the.

Ye sirf 2 mahine hain, par mujhe aisa lagta hai jaise tum hamesha se meri kahani ka hissa thi. Aur sach kahoon to ye kahani abhi shuru hui hai.

Shukriya, Radha. Meri zindagi mein aane ke liye, meri khamoshi ko samajhne ke liye, aur mere har din ko roshan karne ke liye. Tumne mujhe sikhaya ki khush rehne ke liye bahut kuch nahi chahiye hota, bas ek sahi insaan chahiye hota hai.

Main tumse bahut pyaar karta hoon, Radha.
